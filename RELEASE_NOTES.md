# Cartwheel 0.1.3 preview

A more comfortable way to switch modes and save games, with two distinct appearances.

- **Wider mode tabs:** Roulette and Game of the Week now share the full top row, with both names visible on 4:3 and 16:9 screens.
- **Favorite your picks:** the main action is now Favorite / Favorited, alongside Spin again or Reroll. The heart in the header opens your favorites. Favoriting does not mark a game played.
- **Calm Blue by default:** midnight navy, soft blue accents and clean typography. Existing lime-theme installations switch to Calm Blue automatically.
- **Casino appearance:** deep green felt, gold, crimson, serif titles and a roulette-wheel backdrop.
- **Local favorite control:** ES-DE favorites can be unfavorited in Cartwheel without modifying ES-DE's files. Favorite changes survive restarts and rescans.

Update from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**, with **Include preview versions** enabled. Download the update and tap **Install update**; Android will ask you to confirm. You can also install `Cartwheel-0.1.3-preview.apk` directly.

This APK uses the same signing key as earlier previews. Your library, favorite choices, filters and update settings stay in place. Earlier saved play lists remain available in Settings. ROMs and ES-DE files are untouched.

Includes both library import methods, automatic ES-DE box art, online weekly recommendations, unlimited rerolls and the in-app updater. Android 8.0+; landscape 4:3 through 16:9. Android's installer still needs hands-on testing on your handheld.

All 55 automated tests passed. Validation includes tab sizing on 4:3 and 16:9, both appearances, local and imported favorite toggles, persistence and migration from the old theme, plus the existing updater and library tests. APK build, lint and signing checks passed.
